# Vantage — 90-Second Demo Script

Voiceover pacing target: ~150 wpm. Ten scenes, one per visible segment
in `web.json` (each resume → pause pair). Total ≈ 210 words → ~85–90s
with the pauses marked in the recording config.

## Scene 1 — Login / Hook
Selling online usually means juggling ten tools — one for orders, one for stock, a spreadsheet to guess what to reorder.

## Scene 2 — Dashboard Tour
Vantage puts all of it on one screen. Every order lands here with its status at a glance, and the weekly chart shows the shape of the week.

## Scene 3 — Orders Search
Search anything: type "wireless" and the list narrows as you type. No saving, no refreshing — it just happens.

## Scene 4 — Low-Stock Warning
Your stock watches itself. When something runs low, Vantage flags it before you oversell.

## Scene 5 — Fix the Stock
Fix the number, and the warning clears on its own.

## Scene 6 — Add a Product
Adding a product takes one short form and a click.

## Scene 7 — Product Created
New products appear instantly — no reload, no waiting.

## Scene 8 — Demand Forecast
Here is the part sellers usually pay extra for: Vantage studies a month of your sales and predicts the next ones. The shaded band shows how confident it is — so you reorder before you run out, not after.

## Scene 9 — Under the Hood
Under the hood this is built like a product, not a prototype: automated tests guard every flow, releases ship through an automated pipeline, and every shop's data stays private.

## Scene 10 — Call to Action
Vantage is real, working software, and you can poke at it yourself. Try the live demo — the link is in the README below.
